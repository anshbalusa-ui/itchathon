## Body capture procedure

The intended capture setup is a **single default phone placement** with the phone stationary while the person turns as guided. Do not require the person to walk around the phone, reposition the phone to a second placement, enter measurements, complete two full turns, or satisfy a guessed photo/depth count. Follow the actual app guidance and stop if the capture cannot proceed; record the observed screen and recovery state without weakening admission rules. Current UI route and capture requirements must be checked against the integration candidate before an operator begins, because the historical PR #28 procedure described a different scanner.

For self-guided capture, secure iPhone upright with its rear camera facing the subject, arm Start before stepping into view and let the five-second countdown finish. Waiting for valid fresh framing is intentional, not a reason to force completion. A limited room must allow complete usable evidence; reposition/retry if it does not. Do not substitute an unsupported camera/lens or an inferred partial body. These are future test instructions, not a claim that this procedure was performed.
