Only the source OBJ's `body` geometry group is converted. Joint-helper cubes, eyes, eyelash/hair/genital/teeth helpers, tights, and all non-body groups are excluded. Source +Y remains renderer +Y, normalized to a 1.80 m floor-standing body, with +Z facing forward. The body remains the source's smooth detailed base topology. Shirt, vest, and modest shorts are first-party geometry produced by offsetting the complete body along its smooth normals, clipping reviewed garment regions with planar boundaries, and welding their shared edges; they are not third-party garment or retailer assets. The converter is Scripts/build_human_showcase.py and uses only Python's standard library. It does not import or copy MakeHuman program code (which is AGPL).

Payload
-------
DevelopmentAssets/HumanShowcase/human-showcase-v1.bin
Format: FCHUMAN1 version 1, little-endian; four indexed meshes (body, shirt, vest, shorts), 32-bit float positions/normals, UInt16 triangle indices, and authored per-vertex illustrative shell-spacing values on the three garments. Continuous sample offsets range from 5 mm at the chest/shoulders to 30 mm at the waist, with up to 22 mm sleeve room; modest shorts use 12–16 mm. These authored normal offsets are interpolated where garment boundaries clip the source triangles. Values represent only this sample geometry, not personal body measurements, pressure, or verified fit.
